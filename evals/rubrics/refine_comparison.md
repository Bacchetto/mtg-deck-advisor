# Comparing two refines of the same deck

You're shown a request to change a saved Commander deck, the deck as it was, and two changes made to it, A and B. Each change lists the cards cut and added, with their text, and what happened to the land count and the mana curve.

**Decide which change better does what was asked.**
- Judge the change against the request alone: does it make the change asked for, as fully as the pool allows, and only that change?
- When the request asks to cut the weakest cards, judge whether the cards cut are weaker than the ones left in the starting deck.
- When it asks for more of something (removal, card draw, creatures), judge whether the cards added really are that, and good of their kind.
- Don't prefer a change for being bigger, for its order, or for problems the starting deck already had that the request didn't ask to fix.

**Answer `A`, `B`, or `same`** when neither is clearly better, with one sentence naming the cards or counts that decided it.
